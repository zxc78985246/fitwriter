This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Service and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

Definitions

Application refers to FitWriter. Company refers to FitWriter. Country refers to Taiwan. You means the individual accessing or using the Service.

Health Connect Data

This Application requests the following Health Connect permissions:

  • Read Steps — used solely to avoid duplicate entries when writing step data.
  • Write Steps — used to record step count data to Health Connect on behalf of the user.
  • Write Distance — used to record distance data corresponding to the entered steps.

All health data written through this Application is stored locally on Your device via Android Health Connect. We do not collect, transmit, or store any health data on external servers.

Google Account

This Application uses Google Sign-In to identify users and manage usage quota. We only access Your email address for this purpose. No Google account information is transmitted to or stored on any external server.

Local Storage

The following data is stored locally on Your device via SharedPreferences and is never uploaded to any server: usage quota records, login date records, advertisement view count records, PRO membership status, and write history records.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect Advertising IDs, device identifiers, and general location data. You can opt-out of personalized advertising in your device settings under Google Settings > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Google Play Billing

PRO membership purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 16.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.
